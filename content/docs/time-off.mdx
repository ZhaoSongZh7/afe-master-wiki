How to take time off, how it adds up, and which days the program observes.

## How to request time off

1. **Submit via A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → Time → Request time off
2. **Give reasonable notice** — at least 1 week for planned time off; sooner is better for multi-day absences
3. **Notify your manager** — send a quick Slack message or mention it in your 1:1 before submitting
4. **Manager approval** — your manager approves or discusses scheduling via A to Z

<Callout title="Plan around milestones">
  Check the [Program Overview & Timeline](/docs/program-overview) before booking time off. Avoid scheduling absences around your midpoint or final evaluation.
</Callout>

## PTO for interns

- Interns accrue **paid personal time** based on hours worked
- Accrual rate and cap vary by location — check A to Z for your specific balance
- **Use it or lose it** — accrued PTO generally does not carry over or get paid out at the end of the internship. Use what you've earned.
- Unpaid time off may also be available for special circumstances — discuss with your manager

## Holiday calendar (US, 2026)

Amazon observes the following US holidays when offices are closed:

| Holiday | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Independence Day | Friday, July 3 (observed) |
| Labor Day | Monday, September 1 |

Your specific site may observe additional holidays or floating days. Check with your manager or look at your team's calendar.
