How to take time off, how it works, and which days the program observes.

## How to request time off

1. **Get verbal/written approval from your manager first** — discuss the dates before submitting anything in the system
2. **Submit via A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → Time → Request time off
3. **Give reasonable notice** — at least 1 week for planned absences; more for multi-day trips
4. **Manager approves** in the system after your conversation

<Callout type="warn" title="Manager approval required">
  Always get your manager's approval **before** submitting or taking any time off. This is required, not optional.
</Callout>

## PTO policy for interns

AUTA does not own PTO policies — they're managed by HR. Key points:

- Review the [Paid Time Off Policy](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/content/inside/us/en/employment/policies/paid-time-off.html) for **Class - I (interns)** specific to your location
- Accrual rates and entitlements vary by country and employment classification
- Check your available balance in [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → Time
- For detailed PTO support, use [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing)

### Important rules

- **Full-time commitment** — internships are full-time positions (Monday–Friday, 40 hours/week). Interns are expected to be fully available during business hours without external commitments (classes, other jobs).
- **Non-Amazon events** — attending a university event, conference, or personal commitment during work hours requires PTO. There is no "free pass" for school-related activities.
- **Use it or lose it** — accrued PTO generally does not pay out at end of internship. Use what you've earned before your last day.
- **Leave of Absence (LOA)** — interns may be eligible for certain LOA types depending on location. Contact [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing) for guidance.

### Regional specifics

| Region | PTO details |
| --- | --- |
| **US** | Accrued personal time based on hours worked. Submit through A to Z. |
| **Australia / NZ** | Annual Leave + Personal & Carer's Leave + Public Holidays. Accrued per duration of internship. Submit through [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/). |
| **Brazil** | 2.5 paid vacation days per month worked. Minimum 5 days per request. Submit through [ApData](https://apdata.com.br/amazon/main.html). Must schedule 30 days prior to desired leave. |
| **EMEA** | Review your contract for annual leave entitlement. Check balance in A to Z. |
| **LATAM (long-term)** | Interns may adjust working hours by half (15 hrs/week) during exam seasons. Discuss with your manager in advance. |

## Holiday calendar

Amazon observes national holidays where offices are closed. Holidays vary by country.

### US observed holidays (Summer 2026)

| Holiday | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Independence Day | Friday, July 3 (observed) |
| Labor Day | Monday, September 1 |

### Finding holidays for your location

- Visit the [Global National Holidays](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/us/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/taking-time-off/national-holidays.html) page on Inside Amazon
- Navigate to your country/location for the full holiday list
- **Holiday pay**: in the US, hourly interns who work on an observed holiday are typically paid at 1.5× their hourly rate. Check your location's policy for eligibility.

## Exam periods (long-term interns)

If you're on a long-term internship and have exams:

- Proactively work with your manager to adjust your working hours during exam periods
- In LATAM, interns are legally permitted to reduce hours by half (15 hrs/week, 3 hrs/day) during exam seasons
- Normal intern hours in LATAM: 30 hrs/week (6 hrs/day) during business hours (M–F, 8 AM – 6 PM)

## Planning around program milestones

Before booking time off, cross-check the [Program Overview & Timeline](/docs/program-overview):

- **Avoid time off around your midpoint review** (~50% mark) and **final evaluation** (~90% mark)
- **First week** — you should not take time off during onboarding
- **Final two weeks** — avoid absences; you need this time for offboarding, final presentation, and expense submission
