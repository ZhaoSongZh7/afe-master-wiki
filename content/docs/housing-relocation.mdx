## Relocation support

Amazon provides relocation assistance for interns who need to move for the internship:

- **Relocation stipend** — a one-time payment to help cover moving and housing costs. The amount is specified in your offer letter.
- **Temporary housing** — some locations offer corporate housing options or housing lists. Check your offer details or ask [AUTA Intern Program](mailto:internprogram@amazon.com).
- **Travel reimbursement** — one-way travel to your internship location may be covered. Confirm details with your offer letter or HR.

For detailed housing and relocation FAQs, see the [AUTA Housing & Relocation Guidance](https://w.amazon.com/bin/view/University/InternProgram/Interns/Support/Benefits/).

## Finding housing

### Tips from past interns

- **Start early** — housing near Amazon offices fills up fast during intern season (May–June)
- **Check intern housing groups** — past interns share leads in Slack channels and the [AFE Intern Community on Gather](https://gather.a2z.com/group/ef3362df-4500-4b6f-9954-9a324a9c)
- **Proximity to transit** — factor in your commute. See [Transportation & Commute](/docs/transportation-commute) for rideshare and transit options
- **Furnished apartments** — short-term furnished rentals are popular for summer interns (Furnished Finder, Airbnb monthly stays, corporate housing)
- **Roommates** — many interns pair up. Use Gather or Slack to find others interning in the same city

### Location-specific resources

Ask your manager or onboarding buddy about neighborhood recommendations for your specific office location. They'll know which areas are popular with the team.

| Milestone | When |
| --- | --- |
| Offer acceptance + relocation info | Typically 2–3 months before start |
| Secure housing | At least 4 weeks before start |
| Arrive at location | 1–2 days before Day 1 (give yourself time to settle) |
| First day | See your start-date email for exact date |
