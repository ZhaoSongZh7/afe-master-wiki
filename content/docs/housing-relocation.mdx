If you're moving for the program, here's everything you need to know about housing, travel, and getting settled.

<Callout type="warn" title="In-office requirement">
  All interns are expected to relocate to reside within **commuting distance** of their assigned office and attend work in-person a minimum of **five days per week**, the same as full-time employees. You should only accept your offer if you can commit to being within commuting distance by your start date.
</Callout>

## Relocation benefits overview

Amazon provides relocation assistance for interns who need to move for the internship. Your specific benefits are detailed in your **offer letter**. Common relocation support includes:

| Benefit | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Housing stipend** | One-time payment to offset housing costs during your internship (amount in your offer letter) |
| **Corporate intern housing** | Pre-arranged furnished housing through Amazon's provider — 3Sixty |
| **Airfare / travel** | One-way flight to your internship location (booked through BCD Travel) |
| **Rental car** | Available in select locations where public transit is limited (booked through BCD Travel / AVIS) |

<Callout type="info">
  Not all benefits apply to every location. Check your offer letter for what's included in your specific package.
</Callout>

## Housing options

### Option 1: Amazon-arranged intern housing (3Sixty)

Amazon partners with **3Sixty** to provide furnished intern housing in many locations. If your offer includes corporate housing:

- Housing is **pre-arranged** — you'll receive a confirmation with your address, move-in date, and details
- Units are typically **furnished apartments** shared with other interns
- Utilities and basic furnishings are included
- Locations are selected to be within commuting distance of Amazon offices

**3Sixty contact information:**
- Phone: **888-389-0500**
- Email: [Support@Stay3Sixty.com](mailto:Support@Stay3Sixty.com)

### Option 2: Self-arranged housing (stipend)

If you receive a housing stipend instead of corporate housing, you'll find your own accommodation. Tips:

- **Start early** — housing near Amazon offices fills up fast during intern season (May–June). Begin searching 6–8 weeks before your start date.
- **Popular platforms**: Furnished Finder, Airbnb (monthly stays), Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, university housing boards
- **Find roommates** — many interns pair up to save costs. Use the [AFE Intern Community on Gather](https://gather.a2z.com/group/ef3362df-4500-4b6f-9954-9a324a9c) or intern Slack channels to find others in your city.
- **Look near transit** — factor in your daily commute. See [Transportation & Commute](/docs/transportation-commute).

### Important travel rules

- Interns are **NOT eligible for corporate travel cards**. Travel-related expenses are out-of-pocket, then submitted for reimbursement via **Concur**.
- You may use the company card on file for flights (per standard process).
- For hotels: some do not allow travelers under a certain age. Contact Amazon's travel team for support if this applies to you.
- **No travel in your first or final weeks.** First week = onboarding tasks. Final two weeks = offboarding + time to submit expenses for approval.

## Expense reimbursement for relocation

- Submit travel and relocation expenses through **Concur** ([Concur Training](https://www.concurtraining.com/toolkit/en/getting-started/end-user))
- Keep all **itemized receipts** (flights, lodging, food during travel)
- Submit expenses **before your final two weeks** — you need time for review/approval before your internship ends
- Amazon does **not** reimburse for home office furniture (desk, chair, printer, etc.) unless it's a medical accommodation
- For more details on what's reimbursable: see [Expenses & Reimbursement](/docs/expenses-reimbursement)

## Remote work exceptions

The default is **in-office 5 days per week**. Remote work exceptions require:

1. Discussion with your manager and HR Business Partner (HRBP)
2. Formal request through the [Remote Work Exception (RWE) Global Policy](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/content/inside/us/en/employment/getting-hr-help-global/global-offsite-work-arrangements.html)

This is rare for interns — the in-person experience is a core part of the program.
