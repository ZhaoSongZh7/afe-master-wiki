## Before you go

- **Parking passes:** Many trailheads need a [Discover Pass](https://www.discoverpass.wa.gov/)
  (state land) or [Northwest Forest Pass](https://www.fs.usda.gov/detail/r6/passes-permits/recreation)
  (national forest). "None" means no pass required.
- **The 10 essentials:** navigation, water, food, layers, first aid, headlamp,
  sun protection, knife, fire, shelter. Even short hikes.
- **Tell someone** your plan, and check the forecast — mountain weather changes fast.

<Callout type="info">
  Trail data is approximate and for planning only. Always confirm current
  conditions, permits, and closures on [WTA](https://www.wta.org) or AllTrails
