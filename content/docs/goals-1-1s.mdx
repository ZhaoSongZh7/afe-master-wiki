How goals work and how to make the most of your 1:1s.

## Setting goals

Your intern project plan doubles as your goal framework. On Day 1, your manager will walk you through your **Embark plan** which includes:

- **Project deliverables** broken into milestones with clear timelines
- **Technical learning goals** — skills you'll develop during the internship
- **Leadership Principle (LP) development** — behavioral goals tied to Amazon's culture

Revisit these goals at your midpoint review and adjust as needed.

## Running effective 1:1s

### Scheduling

**Pro tip:** Book your 1:1s with your manager for **Monday mornings**. Starting the week with clear priorities helps you avoid scrambling and ensures you're aligned on what matters most.

Most managers hold weekly 30-minute 1:1s. If your manager offers flexibility on timing, Monday mornings are ideal for:
- Setting weekly priorities
- Unblocking issues before they escalate
- Getting feedback while work is still fresh

### What to bring

Come prepared with:
- **Progress updates** — what you shipped, what's in progress, what's blocked
- **Questions** — technical, process, or career-related
- **Feedback requests** — on your work, your approach, or your growth areas

Don't wait for your manager to drive the agenda. Own it.

### What to discuss

- **Project status** — are you on track? Do you need help?
- **Blockers** — anything slowing you down
- **Learning** — what are you picking up? What do you want to learn next?
- **Feedback** — ask for it regularly, not just at midpoint and final reviews
- **Career questions** — your manager is a resource for understanding Amazon and the industry

### How often

Weekly is standard. If you need more frequent check-ins (especially early on), ask. If you're blocked or stuck, don't wait for the next scheduled 1:1 — Slack your manager.

## Asking for feedback

Don't wait for formal reviews. Ask specific questions:
- "How could I have approached that design review differently?"
- "What's one thing I should focus on improving this week?"
- "Am I communicating enough in standups?"

Specific questions get specific, actionable answers.
