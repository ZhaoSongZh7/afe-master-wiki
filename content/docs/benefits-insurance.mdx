Your coverage options and how to enroll.

<Callout type="warn" title="Don't miss enrollment">
  Benefits enrollment opens when you start and closes within your first 30 days. Mark the deadline in your calendar on Day 1.
</Callout>

## What's covered

Amazon provides comprehensive benefits to interns, including:

- **Health insurance** — medical coverage including preventive care, prescriptions, and specialist visits
- **Dental** — preventive and basic dental care
- **Vision** — eye exams and corrective lenses

Coverage details and plan options vary by location. Review your specific options through A to Z once your account is active.

## How to enroll

1. **Log into A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → Benefits section
2. **Review plan options** — compare coverage levels, premiums, and provider networks
3. **Select your plans** — choose health, dental, and vision separately
4. **Confirm enrollment** — submit before the deadline (typically 30 days from start date)

If you miss the enrollment window, you generally cannot enroll until a qualifying life event. Contact HR immediately if you're approaching the deadline.

## Payroll basics

- **Direct deposit** — set up via A to Z with your bank routing and account numbers
- **Pay schedule** — biweekly for most US-based employees
- **Tax forms** — W-4 (withholding) and state tax forms submitted during onboarding
- **Pay stubs** — viewable in A to Z under Payroll
