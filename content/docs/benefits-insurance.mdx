Your coverage options, payroll basics, and how to get help.

<Callout type="warn" title="Don't miss enrollment">
  Benefits enrollment opens when you start and closes within your first **30 days**. Mark the deadline in your calendar on Day 1. If you miss it, you generally cannot enroll until a qualifying life event.
</Callout>

## What's covered

Amazon provides benefits to eligible interns, including:

- **Health insurance** — medical coverage including preventive care, prescriptions, and specialist visits
- **Dental** — preventive and basic dental care
- **Vision** — eye exams and corrective lenses

<Callout type="info" title="Eligibility varies">
  Intern benefits eligibility varies by location, role, and employment classification. AUTA recommends reviewing benefits and time-off information on the [Global Benefits page](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/pl/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/benefits-global.html) on Inside Amazon. Use [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr) for personalized support.
</Callout>

## How to enroll

1. **Log into A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → Benefits section
2. **Review plan options** — compare coverage levels, premiums, and provider networks for your location
3. **Select your plans** — choose health, dental, and vision separately
4. **Confirm enrollment** — submit before the 30-day deadline from your start date

## Payroll & getting paid

### Key details

| Topic | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Pay frequency** | Biweekly (US); varies by country — check [Getting Paid (Global)](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/content/inside/us/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/compensation-global/getting-paid-global.html) |
| **Direct deposit** | Set up via [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) with your bank routing and account numbers during onboarding |
| **Pay stubs** | Viewable in A to Z → Payroll |
| **Tax forms** | W-4 (federal withholding) and state tax forms submitted during onboarding |
| **Holiday pay (US)** | Hourly interns who work on an observed holiday are typically paid at 1.5× their hourly rate |

### Timekeeping

Different intern populations use different timekeeping tools. If you're unsure which tool to use for submitting your hours, reach out to [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr). Common tools:

- [TimeClock Plus](https://amazon.awsapps.com/workdocs/index.html#/document/259e20caf9c425ae2d658ab9c365070c023576c43e1b35029fd480877e9c4074)
- [MyTime](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/pl/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/taking-time-off/mytime.html)
- [Work Events](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/us/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/taking-time-off/work-events.html)

For time corrections (punch corrections, schedule changes, pay code changes), contact [Centralized Timekeeping](https://inside.hr.amazon.dev/pl/en/employment/time-off-compensation-benefits/taking-time-off.html).
