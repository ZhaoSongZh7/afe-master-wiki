How to get your money back for covered expenses, and what qualifies.

<Callout type="warn" title="No corporate travel card">
  Interns are **NOT** eligible for corporate travel cards. All travel-related expenses are paid out-of-pocket and submitted for reimbursement through Concur. The exception: you may use the company card on file for flights (per standard process).
</Callout>

## What's reimbursable

| Category | Covered? | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relocation travel** (flights, lodging, food in transit) | Yes | Must be pre-approved; booked through BCD Travel when possible |
| **Rental car** (where provided) | Yes | Booked through BCD Travel / AVIS |
| **Business travel** (visiting another Amazon office) | Yes | Manager approval required; charges to business cost center |
| **Team events / offsites** | Yes | Usually handled by your manager directly |
| **Daily commute** (gas, transit, rideshare) | No | Regular commuting is not reimbursable (Lyft credits are separate — see [Transportation](/docs/transportation-commute)) |
| **Home office furniture** (desk, chair, printer) | No | Not reimbursable per Amazon policy. Medical accommodations are the exception — request through [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing). |
| **Personal items** | No | Amazon does not reimburse non-company-sponsored events or personal purchases |

## How to submit via Concur

Amazon uses **Concur** for expense reporting. Here's the process:

1. **Access Concur** — log in through [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "Concur" or go directly to the Concur portal
2. **Create an expense report** — select the appropriate expense category
3. **Attach receipts** — upload itemized receipts (must show date, vendor, amount, and items purchased)
4. **Add details** — include business purpose, project/cost center, and any manager pre-approval
5. **Submit for approval** — your manager reviews and approves the report
6. **Reimbursement** — funds are deposited to your bank account on file (typically within 1–2 pay cycles after approval)

<Callout title="Concur training">
  New to Concur? Complete the [Concur End-User Training](https://www.concurtraining.com/toolkit/en/getting-started/end-user) for a walkthrough of creating reports, attaching receipts, and tracking status.
</Callout>

## Travel expenses (detailed)

If you're approved for business travel during your internship (e.g., visiting your team's other office):

### Rules

- Travel must be **pre-approved by your manager** and your manager's VP manages exceptions
- Interns **may not travel** in the first or final weeks of the internship (first week = onboarding; final two weeks = offboarding and expense submission)
- Full expenses charge to the **business cost center** — AUTA does not have a central budget for intern travel
- Book flights through the company card on file; pay for lodging and food out-of-pocket, then submit via Concur

### What to document

- **Flights**: booking confirmation + receipt
- **Hotel**: itemized folio showing nightly rate, taxes, and room charges only (no mini-bar, movies, etc.)
- **Meals**: itemized receipts with date and restaurant name
- **Ground transportation**: receipts for taxis/rideshare to and from airport or meetings

## Tips to get reimbursed faster

- **Submit promptly** — don't wait until the end of the internship. Submit within a few days of the expense.
- **Itemized receipts only** — credit card statements alone are not sufficient
- **One report per trip** — group all expenses from a single trip into one report
- **Include business justification** — a clear one-line reason ("Travel to SEA for team offsite, approved by [manager name]")
- **Check your bank details** — verify your direct deposit info in A to Z before submitting your first expense

## Deadline

Submit all expense reports **before your final two weeks** of the internship. After your end date, you lose access to Concur, and unsubmitted expenses become extremely difficult to process.
