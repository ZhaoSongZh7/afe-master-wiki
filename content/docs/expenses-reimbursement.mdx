How to get your money back for covered expenses, and what qualifies.

## What's covered

Eligible expense categories for interns typically include:

- **Relocation and travel** — pre-approved moving expenses for interns who relocate (see [Housing / Relocation](/docs/housing-relocation))
- **Lyft / rideshare** — program-provided commute benefits (see [Transportation & Commute](/docs/transportation-commute))
- **Equipment** — any approved peripherals or accessories beyond standard issue (see [GREF](/docs/gref))
- **Team events** — expenses for team offsites or social events (usually handled by your manager)

<Callout type="warn" title="Pre-approval required">
  Always confirm with your manager or program coordinator before incurring expenses you expect to be reimbursed. Unauthorized purchases may not be reimbursable.
</Callout>

## How to submit

1. **Go to Amazon A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "expenses" or "reimbursement"
2. **Attach documentation** — itemized receipts showing date, vendor, and amount
3. **Include any required approvals** — manager or program coordinator pre-approval if needed
4. **Submit within the required window** — typically within 60 days of the expense; sooner is better

## Tips to get reimbursed faster

- Keep **itemized receipts** (not just credit card statements) for everything
- Submit expenses as they occur — don't batch them all at the end of the internship
- Include a brief description of the business purpose
- Double-check that your bank account is set up correctly in A to Z

## Common questions

**What if my reimbursement is delayed?**
Check the status in A to Z first. If it's been more than 2 weeks, contact [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing).

**Is food covered?**
Typically not for day-to-day meals, unless you're traveling for work or attending a team event. Check with your manager.

**What about work-from-home equipment?**
If your team has a remote/hybrid arrangement, your manager can approve peripherals through GREF.

<Callout type="info">
  Not sure if something is covered? Ask before you spend — check with your
  [program coordinator](/docs/whos-who) or manager.
</Callout>
