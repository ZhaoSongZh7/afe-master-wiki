Setting up your physical workspace.

## Finding your desk

- **Assigned seating**: most intern teams have a designated area. Your manager or onboarding buddy will show you your spot on Day 1.
- **Hoteling / hot-desking**: some buildings use shared desks that you reserve for the day. Your onboarding buddy can explain the system and show you how to book.
- **Team neighborhoods**: many teams sit together in a designated zone. You'll typically be near your tech mentor and onboarding buddy for easy collaboration.

Ask your manager before Day 1 if you'll have an assigned desk or need to reserve one.

## Desk setup

Your basic workspace typically includes:

| Item | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Laptop | Provided on Day 1 (see [Before Day One](/docs/before-day-one)) |
| External monitor | Available from building stock — request via [GREF](/docs/gref) |
| Keyboard + mouse | Standard issue with laptop, or request preferred peripherals via GREF |
| Docking station | Request via GREF if not pre-provisioned at your desk |
| Headset | For meetings — request via GREF |

Most buildings have **IT vending machines** (Amazon Lockers or similar) where you can self-serve common peripherals with your badge. Ask your onboarding buddy where the nearest one is.

## Ergonomics & adjustments

- **Chair adjustment** — most chairs have multiple adjustment points (height, lumbar, armrests). Take 5 minutes to set yours up properly on Day 1.
- **Monitor height** — top of the screen should be at or slightly below eye level. Use a monitor arm or riser if needed.
- **Standing desk** — available in some buildings. Request through GREF if your desk doesn't have one.
- **Ergonomic assessment** — if you're experiencing discomfort, request an assessment through A to Z (search "ergonomics"). Amazon provides guidance and may supply additional equipment.

## Working remotely

If your team has a hybrid or remote arrangement:

- Discuss expectations with your manager on Day 1 (which days in-office, flexible hours, etc.)
- Remote-day equipment (monitor, peripherals for home) can be requested through GREF with manager approval
- Keep your Cloud Desktop (CDD) setup current so you can work from anywhere with a network connection
