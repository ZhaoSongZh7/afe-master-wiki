## What to Expect

On Day One, your laptop will need to install mandatory security software. **This process can take up to 2 hours.**

## Tips

- **Bring something to read** or other work you can do on your phone
- Don't plan any critical tasks during this time
- The installation is automatic — just let it run
- This is normal and happens to all new hires

## During the Wait

This is a good time to:
- Review onboarding materials
- Read team documentation
- Introduce yourself to nearby teammates
- Familiarize yourself with your workspace
