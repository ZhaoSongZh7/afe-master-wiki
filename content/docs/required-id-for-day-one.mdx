## What to Bring

On your first day, you'll need to provide identification for I-9 verification. **A driver's license alone is not sufficient.**

## Acceptable Options

- **Option 1:** Passport
- **Option 2:** Two forms of ID from the approved I-9 list

## Important Note

Make sure to bring the required documents on Day One, as you cannot complete onboarding without proper identification.

## Additional Resources

Check your Day One email for the complete list of acceptable ID combinations, or refer to the official I-9 documentation.
