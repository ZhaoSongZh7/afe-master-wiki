---
title: Scheduling 1:1s with Your Manager
description: Best practices for timing your weekly 1:1 meetings with your manager.
---

## Recommended Timing

**Schedule your 1:1s for Monday mornings** when possible.

## Why Monday Mornings?

- Start the week with **clear priorities**
- Get alignment on goals before diving into work
- Avoid scrambling mid-week when you're already deep in tasks
- Address blockers early in the week when there's time to resolve them

## General 1:1 Tips

- Come prepared with topics and questions
- Use this time to discuss priorities, not just status updates
- Keep a running doc of items to discuss throughout the week
