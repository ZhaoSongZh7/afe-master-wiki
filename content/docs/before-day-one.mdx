Everything you need to walk in confident on day one.

<Callout title="Quick checklist">
  Government-issued photo ID · signed offer/onboarding paperwork · your start-date
  email with building + time · comfortable clothes.
</Callout>

## What to bring

- **Photo ID** — government-issued; required for badge pickup. **Note:** A driver's license alone may not be sufficient. Bring your **passport or two forms of ID** to ensure a smooth badge issuance process.
- **Signed paperwork** — any offer letter or onboarding documents you were asked to complete
- **Start-date email** — contains your building location, floor, and arrival time
- **Comfortable clothes** — business casual is the norm; see the culture page for more on dress code
- **Something to read** — laptop setup (especially security software installation) can take 1-2 hours on Day 1

## Where to go

Your start-date email will specify:
- Building name and address
- Floor number
- Arrival time (usually 9:00 AM)
- Who to ask for at the front desk

If you're unsure, contact your recruiter or the AFE program team before Day 1.

## What happens on Day 1

1. **Badge pickup** — go to the security desk with your ID
2. **Laptop setup** — IT will provision your machine and install required security software (plan for this to take time)
3. **Onboarding sessions** — HR, benefits, and program overview
4. **Meet your team** — your manager will introduce you and walk you through your workspace

See [Day-One Checklist](/docs/day-one-checklist) for a complete task list.
