Everything you need to walk in confident on day one.

<Callout title="Quick checklist">
  Government-issued photo ID · signed offer/onboarding paperwork · your start-date
  email with building + time · comfortable clothes.
</Callout>

## What to bring

- **Photo ID** — government-issued; required for badge issuance and building access.
- **Onboarding paperwork** — anything HR asked you to complete or sign in advance (offer letter, I-9 documents, etc.).
- **Banking + tax info** — for payroll setup (direct deposit routing number, tax withholding forms).
- **Personal laptop charger** — Amazon will provide a work laptop, but you may want your personal device for the first hour while IT provisions yours.

## Badge & laptop pickup

- Your **start-date email** (sent ~1 week before) will specify the exact building, floor, and time to arrive.
- **Badge**: issued at the security desk on Day 1. Bring your photo ID. Badge grants building access from that moment forward.
- **Laptop / equipment**: your manager or onboarding buddy will walk you through pickup. Most sites provision a MacBook or ThinkPad depending on team. See also [GREF equipment requests](/docs/gref) for additional peripherals.

<Callout type="warn" title="If something isn't ready">
  If your badge or laptop isn't available at the listed location, contact your manager or onboarding buddy immediately. They can reach the IT help desk on your behalf.
</Callout>

## Dress code

Amazon's dress code is casual — wear what makes you comfortable and productive. Jeans, t-shirts, and sneakers are common. No formal attire is expected unless you're visiting a customer site or attending a specific event. Lab spaces may require closed-toe shoes.

## Where to show up

- **Building & room**: specified in your start-date email.
- **Arrival time**: typically 9:00 AM local time on your first day, unless otherwise noted.
- **Who to check in with**: your manager or onboarding buddy will meet you at the lobby. See [Who's Who / Key Contacts](/docs/whos-who) for their details.

## What happens on Day 1

1. Badge and laptop pickup
2. Meet your manager, tech mentor, and onboarding buddy
3. Walk through your **Embark plan** (onboarding roadmap + intern project plan)
4. Complete mandatory training modules (orientation, security basics)
5. Team introductions and workspace setup

Your manager is required to have your Embark plan (including your intern project) ready before your first day. If you haven't received any communication about your project plan by Day 1, ask your manager directly.
