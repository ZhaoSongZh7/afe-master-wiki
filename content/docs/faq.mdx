## Getting started

**Where do I go on my first day?**
Check your start-date email for the exact building, floor, and time. Full details on [Before Day One](/docs/before-day-one).

**What do all these acronyms mean?**
See [Glossary & Acronyms](/docs/glossary-acronyms). You can also search [Acronym Central](https://w.amazon.com/index.php/Acronym_Central).

**What should I focus on in my first week?**
Get set up (badge, laptop, accounts), complete SDE Bootcamp, meet your team (manager, tech mentor, onboarding buddy), and do your first code review. See [Dev Environment Setup](/docs/dev-environment-setup).

**How is AFE different from the regular SDE internship?**
AFE interns are earlier in their academic career (after first year vs. third year). You're evaluated against [AFE Role Guidelines](https://ivy-help-center.talent.a2z.com/article/5CPkBGwcCeMRX9uDckSlEe), not the standard SDE intern bar. You have additional support layers (career mentor, AFE alumni, AFE team). See [Program Overview](/docs/program-overview).

## The internship

**What am I expected to deliver?**
A well-scoped project defined in your Embark plan. Your manager creates the project plan before Day 1. See [Program Overview](/docs/program-overview) for milestones.

**How am I evaluated?**
Midpoint review (~50% mark) and final evaluation at the end. You're assessed on role-specific competencies, Leadership Principles, and learning trajectory. See [Evaluations](/docs/evaluations).

**Am I evaluated for a full-time offer or a return internship?**
Depends on your graduation date. Oct 2027 or later → return internship. Jan 2026 – Sep 2027 → FTE. See [Evaluations](/docs/evaluations).

**What if I'm struggling with my project?**
Talk to your manager and tech mentor immediately. They can descope or provide additional support. Also reach out to AFE alumni via Slack or the [AFE support intake form](https://form.asana.com/?k=q4Kasz4o_vHkk8014_CfLQ&d=8442528107068).

## Money & benefits

**When do I need to enroll in benefits?**
Within your first 30 days. See [Benefits & Insurance](/docs/benefits-insurance).

**How do I get reimbursed?**
Through A to Z. See [Expenses & Reimbursement](/docs/expenses-reimbursement).

**How do I request time off?**
Via A to Z with manager notification. See [Time Off / PTO / Holidays](/docs/time-off).

**When do I get paid?**
Biweekly via direct deposit. Set up your bank info in [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) during onboarding.

**Where can I ask technical questions?**
