## Mental health resources

### tbh (US-based AFE interns)

AFE partners with **tbh** to provide mental health resources specifically for AFE scholars. They offer:

- **Text-based support** — message a counselor anytime
- **Group sessions** — facilitated discussions with peers
- **Individual support** — one-on-one counseling sessions
- **Online resources** — articles, guides, and self-help tools on [tbh.us](https://www.tbh.us/)

tbh is a great resource as you navigate big moments — new city, first corporate job, imposter syndrome, homesickness, and more.

### Additional resources

- [Amazon A to Z wellness resources](https://atoz.amazon.work/) — search "wellness" for current offerings
- Team mental health days or wellness activities (ask your manager about team norms)
- Your **Career Mentor** is available for confidential conversations about navigating challenges

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The **EAP** provides free, confidential support for all Amazon employees including interns:

| Service | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Counseling** | Short-term counseling sessions (in-person, phone, or video) |
| **Referrals** | Connection to longer-term therapists, financial advisors, legal resources |
| **Work-life support** | Help with stress, relationships, adjustment, and more |
| **24/7 availability** | Call anytime — the service is always available |

Access the EAP through [Amazon A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) — search "EAP" or "Employee Assistance Program" for the current provider and contact information.

<Callout title="Confidential">
  EAP conversations are confidential. Your manager, team, and Amazon will not be informed that you used the service.
</Callout>

## Accommodations & accessibility

If you need a workplace accommodation (physical, cognitive, or otherwise):

1. **Start with HR** — submit a request through [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing) or talk to your HR partner
2. **Equipment accommodations** — specialized chairs, desks, monitors, assistive technology → file through [GREF](/docs/gref)
3. **Schedule accommodations** — modified hours, break schedules, etc. → discuss with your manager
4. **Religious accommodations** — prayer space, dietary needs, schedule flexibility → talk to HR

Your manager should be supportive of accommodation requests. If you encounter any difficulty, escalate to the [AFE Program Team](mailto:afe-internship-program@amazon.com).

## Taking care of yourself during the internship

A few reminders:

- **You're not expected to work evenings or weekends.** If your workload feels unmanageable, talk to your manager about scoping.
- **Take your PTO.** See [Time Off](/docs/time-off) for how to request it.
- **Use your support network.** You have a manager, tech mentor, career mentor, alumni, and the AFE team — lean on them. See [Who's Who](/docs/whos-who).
- **It's normal to feel uncertain.** You're in a new environment with new tools and new expectations. That discomfort is part of growth.

- Equipment accommodations: see [GREF](/docs/gref).
- Start with your [HR Partner](/docs/whos-who).
