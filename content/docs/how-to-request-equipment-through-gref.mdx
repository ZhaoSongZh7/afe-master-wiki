## Overview

You can expense monitors and other approved peripherals through GREF (Global Real Estate and Facilities). The process is straightforward and typically takes 3 days for delivery.

## Steps

1. Go to [gref.amazon.com](http://gref.amazon.com)
2. Select **"Peripheral Request"** from the menu
3. Choose your item from the approved list
4. Submit your request

## Timeline

Expect your equipment to arrive within **3 business days** of approval.

## What You Can Order

Only items on the approved list are available through this system. Check the GREF portal for the current catalog of monitors and peripherals.
