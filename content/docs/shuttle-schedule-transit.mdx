## Fremont to SLU Shuttle

### Peak Hours (7-9am, 4-6pm)

Shuttles run **every 15 minutes**

### Off-Peak Hours

Shuttles run **every 30 minutes**

## Additional Information

For real-time updates, route maps, and other transit options, check the **internal transit page** on the company intranet.

## Planning Your Commute

- During peak hours, you have flexibility with minimal wait times
- For off-peak travel, plan ahead to avoid 30-minute waits
- Consider checking the transit page for any schedule changes or service alerts
