Everything about your badge and getting into the building.

## Getting your badge

Your badge is issued at the security desk on **Day 1**. Bring your government-issued photo ID. Badge issuance details (building, floor, time) are in your start-date email. See [Before Day One](/docs/before-day-one) for the full Day 1 checklist.

## Access hours & secured areas

- **Standard access hours**: most Amazon offices are accessible 6 AM – 8 PM local time with your badge. Exact hours vary by building.
- **After-hours access**: many buildings allow 24/7 badge access for employees, but some floors or labs restrict after-hours entry. Check with your manager.
- **Secured areas**: server rooms, labs, and executive floors require additional badge permissions. If your project needs access to a secured area, your manager can request it.
- **Tailgating policy**: never let someone follow you through a secured door without badging themselves in, even if they appear to be an employee.

## Lost or broken badge

1. Report it immediately — email or Slack your manager
2. Visit the **security desk** in your building's lobby for a temporary badge (bring your photo ID)
3. A replacement badge is typically issued same-day
4. If you're locked out after hours and can't reach security, call the building's emergency line (posted near badge readers) or contact your manager

## Visitors

If you're expecting a guest (family, career mentor, etc.):

1. **Register the visitor in advance** through your site's visitor management system (ask your onboarding buddy for the link)
2. **Meet them at the lobby** — visitors cannot badge in unescorted
3. **Escort them at all times** — visitors must be accompanied by a badged employee in all work areas
4. **Sign them out** when they leave
