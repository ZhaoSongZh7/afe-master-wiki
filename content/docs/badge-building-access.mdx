Everything about your badge and getting into the building.

## Getting your badge

Your badge is issued at the security desk on **Day 1**. Bring your government-issued photo ID. Badge issuance details (building, floor, time) are in your start-date email. See [Before Day One](/docs/before-day-one) for what to bring.

## Building access hours

Most Amazon buildings are accessible 24/7 with your badge, but some floors or areas may have restricted hours. Check with your manager or building security if you plan to work outside standard hours (7 AM - 7 PM).

## What to do if your badge stops working

### After a building move or transfer

If your badge stops working after moving to a new building or team, go to the **security desk on floor 1** (NOT the IT help desk). Security can reactivate your badge in about 2 minutes.

### Lost or damaged badge

1. Report it immediately to building security
2. Request a replacement at the security desk
3. There may be a replacement fee for lost badges (check with HR)

### Badge not working at turnstiles

- Make sure you're tapping the correct reader
- Check that your badge isn't demagnetized (keep it away from phones and magnets)
- If it still doesn't work, visit the security desk

## Tailgating policy

Never hold the door open for someone without a badge, even if they look familiar. Everyone must badge in individually. This is a security requirement, not rudeness.

## Visitor access

Interns cannot sponsor visitors or guests. If you need to bring someone on-site, coordinate with your manager.
