How evaluations work and what to expect.

## Timeline

Evaluations happen at two key points:

| Milestone | When | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Midpoint review** | ~50% of internship | Formal performance discussion; share trends, strengths, and growth areas; set action items for the second half |
| **Final evaluation (IEM)** | End of internship (~90% mark) | Intern Evaluation Meeting to determine whether you raise the bar for a return offer |

Your manager will deliver feedback at both points. Neither evaluation should come as a surprise — you and your manager (and tech mentor) should have a shared understanding of your performance and progress throughout the summer via regular 1:1s.

## Criteria — Raising the bar

You are evaluated against:

1. **Role-specific technical and functional competencies** aligned with the [AFE Role Guidelines](https://ivy-help-center.talent.a2z.com/article/5CPkBGwcCeMRX9uDckSlEe)
2. **Amazon Leadership Principles** behaviors demonstrated at the interviewing level
3. **Current demonstrated abilities**, with appropriate acknowledgment of your early career stage

<Callout title="The AFE standard">
  AFE scholars are not expected to consistently demonstrate full-time employee behaviors by the end of their first internship. The question is: *Does this scholar demonstrate the curiosity, ownership, and foundational aptitude that signal they are on a trajectory to raise Amazon's bar, and would a second internship accelerate that trajectory?*
</Callout>

### Functional competencies

Your manager selects up to three relevant [functional competencies](https://w.amazon.com/bin/view/TalentAssessment/InterviewResearch/InterviewRatingGuides/Functional/) based on your role. These are evaluated using the associated calibration guide alongside the AFE Role Guidelines.

### What "raising the bar" means for each offer type

- **Return internship**: Demonstrate competencies and capabilities that would raise the bar against the current full-time population, acknowledging your early career state. The first internship is a data point in ongoing development, not a final assessment.
- **Full-time (FTE)**: Raise Amazon's hiring bar against the current full-time employment population at the time of conversion.

## Midpoint review

During the midpoint meeting, your manager will:

- Discuss performance in your role and level, using the role guidelines
- Share observable strengths and growth areas based on Leadership Principles
- Share your current **performance trend** (improving, meeting expectations, or below)
- Collaborate on an **action plan** with clear next steps for the second half

<Callout type="warn" title="Prepare ahead">
  Review your project progress, gather examples of your work, and think about where you want to grow before the midpoint meeting. Use the [1:1 Conversation Guide](https://amazon.awsapps.com/workdocs-amazon/index.html#/document/55389262afdc1c6199623903b5c373f053e132379d172a3f77ee0c72fb1888fd) to prepare.
</Callout>

## Final evaluation (IEM)

The **Intern Evaluation Meeting (IEM)** is the formal end-of-internship review:

| Participant | Role in IEM |
| --- | --- |
| **Manager** | Presents the evaluation document; leads the discussion |
| **Mentor** | Provides feedback on technical growth and day-to-day performance |
| **Bar Raiser** | Required for SDE FTE and MBA evaluations only (not for return internship evaluations) |

### Manager preparation checklist

1. Complete the [Intern Manager Onboarding training](http://atoz.amazon.work/learn/trainings/CRSLERN20251125194703ccb7179a?lms=LEARN) (MEOT module)
2. Ensure [Making Great Hiring Decisions (MGHD)](https://atoz.amazon.work/m/learn/transcriptdetail?trainingId=TCRLERN20250415191438f2e2d535&trainingLms=LEARN) compliance
3. Request feedback from mentor and stakeholders in the [Intern Evaluation Form (IEF)](https://atoz.amazon.work/intern-evaluation-form)
4. Send completed IEF to required stakeholders **48 hours before the IEM**
5. Lead the evaluation discussion and input the outcome into Hire during the meeting

## How feedback is delivered

- **Ongoing**: Weekly 1:1s with your manager and regular check-ins with your tech mentor
- **Midpoint**: Formal sit-down with documented strengths, growth areas, and action items
- **Final**: IEM outcome communicated by your manager after the meeting

Headcount constraints should **never** factor into your performance assessment.
