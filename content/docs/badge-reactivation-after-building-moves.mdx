---
title: Badge Reactivation After Building Moves
description: Where to go when your badge stops working after changing buildings.
---

## Problem

If your badge stops working after you move to a new building, it needs to be reactivated.

## Solution

**Go to the Security Desk on Floor 1** — NOT the IT Help Desk.

## Timeline

The security team can reactivate your badge in approximately **2 minutes**.

## Why This Happens

Badge access permissions are tied to building assignments. When you move buildings, your badge access needs to be updated in the security system.
