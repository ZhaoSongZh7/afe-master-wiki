Make the most of the program: leverage your support network, set goals, and grow.

## Your support network

AFE interns have access to multiple layers of support beyond the standard intern experience:

| Role | Who they are | How they help |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Manager** | Your host team manager | Project design, weekly 1:1s, evaluations, resource allocation |
| **Tech Mentor** | Senior engineer on your team | Day-to-day technical questions, code reviews, skill development |
| **Onboarding Buddy** | Teammate | Day 1 orientation, team culture, Slack channels, bookmarks |
| **Career Mentor** | Volunteer Amazonian outside your team | Professional growth, navigating Amazon culture, confidential guidance |
| **AFE Alumni** | Full-time SDEIs/SDEIIs and return interns | Peer support via Slack AMA channel, office hours, debugging sessions |
| **AFE Program Team** | AFE staff | Pre-internship prep, curated resources, in-person events, escalation support |

<Callout title="Career Mentor confidentiality">
  Career mentor conversations are completely separate from your host team. They're a great resource if you want to discuss something in confidence.
</Callout>

## Growth paths

### The AFE trajectory

AFE is designed as a **ladder up** to the SDE (or other) intern role. Your first internship is a data point in ongoing development, not a final assessment:

1. **First AFE internship** → foundational skills, learn the tools, deliver a scoped project
2. **Return internship** → deeper technical ownership, more complex scope, FTE-track evaluation
3. **Full-time offer** → evaluated against the standard Amazon hiring bar at graduation

### What "good" looks like for AFE

The most important Leadership Principle for AFE interns is **Learn and Be Curious**. You're evaluated on:

- A visible **learning trajectory** — curiosity, ownership, and foundational aptitude
- Role-specific competencies from the [AFE Role Guidelines](https://ivy-help-center.talent.a2z.com/article/5CPkBGwcCeMRX9uDckSlEe)
- Demonstrated Leadership Principles behaviors at the appropriate level

## Setting goals

Your manager will work with you on Day 1 to review your project plan and set expectations. Goals should be:

- **Specific** — tied to concrete deliverables in your project plan
- **Time-bound** — mapped to the internship milestones (midpoint, final)
- **Role-appropriate** — scoped to your level per AFE Role Guidelines

Use the [Intern Roadmap Infographic](https://amazon.awsapps.com/workdocs-amazon/index.html#/document/b9f5eb75a4b6965790c3cab9743437dc7a4c17c88fc289f584266839b8b471a3) to understand key milestones across the internship.

## Making the most of mentorship

### With your tech mentor

- Set a preferred communication method early (Slack, ad-hoc meetings, etc.)
- Bring specific questions — "I tried X and got stuck at Y" works better than "I'm stuck"
- Use them for code reviews, architecture discussions, and debugging
- Share your learning goals so they can tailor guidance

### With your career mentor

- Meet regularly (bi-weekly is common)
- Discuss professional development, Amazon culture, career paths
- Ask about their own journey — what they wish they'd known
- Use them as a sounding board for tough conversations

### With AFE Alumni

- Join the AFE Slack AMA channel (you're auto-added)
- Attend alumni office hours for technical or professional questions
- Reach out directly for coffee/lunch — they've been in your shoes
