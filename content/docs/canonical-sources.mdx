## Freshness and privacy
The consolidated AFE Intern Home replaces deprecated manager training. Do not use old 2024 calendars or in-person onboarding pages for 2026 dates. The current support model emphasizes alumni office hours and on-demand support.

Never export Discord or private conversations into Relay without explicit AFE program-owner, Privacy, Legal, and Security approval. Do not use third-party browser exporters or store personal data, private conversations, or Amazon-confidential information in a personal database.
