The shape of the AFE internship from start to finish.

## Program at a glance

The **Amazon Future Engineer (AFE) Scholarship and Internship** program has been breaking down barriers for underserved students pursuing careers in tech since 2017 in the US and 2022 in India. It combines multi-year financial aid, comprehensive wraparound support, and an early internship opportunity at Amazon. AFE scholars intern after their **first year of college** (compared to traditional third-year interns), bringing fresh energy, Gen Z insights, and diverse experiences to their teams.

Every AFE intern has completed at least **CS101** or an equivalent foundational course and is familiar with one or more programming languages. No prior industry experience is required or expected at the time of their first internship.

<Callout title="Results">
  100% of AFE's first three graduating classes completed their degrees, 92% are now working in tech careers or pursuing advanced degrees, and 125+ AFE alumni work full-time at Amazon as SDEs (as of Feb 2026).
</Callout>

## Timeline & milestones

The internship follows a compressed timeline. Key phases:

| Phase | Timing | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-Day-One preparation | 4 weeks before start | Manager creates Embark plan + project plan; mentor + buddy assigned |
| Onboarding (Weeks 1–2) | First 2 weeks | Embark plan, SDE Bootcamp, team environment setup, first code review |
| Ramp & first task (Week 2–3) | Weeks 2–3 | First coding task from team backlog, project design sketching |
| Project execution (Weeks 3–10) | Core of internship | Design doc → implementation → iterative CRs → feedback cycles |
| Midpoint evaluation | ~50% mark | Formal performance discussion with manager; midpoint trend shared |
| Final evaluation | ~90% mark | Intern Evaluation Meeting (IEM) with manager + mentor |
| Wrap-up & exit | Final week | Presentation, knowledge transfer, offboarding |

## Completion requirements

- **Deliver your intern project** — complete the deliverables defined in your project plan, scoped to your role and level per the [AFE Role Guidelines](https://ivy-help-center.talent.a2z.com/article/5CPkBGwcCeMRX9uDckSlEe).
- **Demonstrate a learning trajectory** — curiosity, foundational aptitude, and ownership behaviors that signal continued growth.
- **Complete required trainings** — Intern Onboarding Training, SDE Bootcamp, and any team-specific modules assigned in Embark.
- **Participate in evaluations** — midpoint and final evaluation meetings.

## Evaluation eligibility

| Graduation date | Offer eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| Jan 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027 | Full-time employee (FTE) evaluation |
| Oct 1, 2027 or later | Return internship evaluation |

AFE scholars are evaluated for a **return internship**, not a full-time offer (unless their graduation date qualifies them for FTE). The evaluation standard reflects their stage of development. See [Evaluations](/docs/evaluations) for the full process.
