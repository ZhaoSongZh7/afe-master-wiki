## Intern events

this is a test

AUTA and AFE host events throughout the summer to build community and celebrate intern culture. Events are shared via:

- **Intern newsletters** — sent at key milestones (welcome, 25%, midpoint, 75%, exit)
- **Gather** — Amazon's internal community platform. Join the [AFE Intern Community on Gather](https://gather.a2z.com/group/ef3362df-4500-4b6f-9954-9a324a9c)
- **Manager Gather page** — managers receive event info via the [Manager Community](https://gather.a2z.com/group/b4e41a8e-e4d2-43f3-8e30-ca516c529a15)
- **Slack channels** — AFE-specific channels and the broader intern community channels

### Types of events

| Category | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **AFE-specific** | Bootcamp, cohort meet-ups, in-person engagements, alumni office hours |
| **AUTA global** | Intern Day, community service events, career panels, networking mixers |
| **Team-level** | Team socials, lunch & learns, sprint demos |
| **Learning** | Design review workshops, leadership talks on Broadcast |

## Affinity & resource groups

Amazon has numerous employee resource groups (affinity groups) open to interns:

- **Together Hub** ([atoz.amazon.work/together](https://atoz.amazon.work/together)) — discover and join affinity groups across Amazon
- **Glamazon** — LGBTQ+ community
- **Black Employee Network (BEN)**
- **Latinos@Amazon**
- **Women in Engineering**
- **AmazonPWD** — People with Disabilities
- And many more — browse the Together Hub for the full list

<Callout title="Join early">
  Affinity groups are a great way to expand your network beyond your host team. Many host their own events, mentorship programs, and Slack communities.
</Callout>

## AFE Alumni Network

The AFE alumni community is one of the strongest parts of the program. Alumni are previous AFE interns who are now full-time SDEIs, SDEIIs, or return interns at Amazon.

### How to connect with alumni

| Channel | What it offers |
| --- | --- |
| **Slack AMA channel** | Ask any question (technical or professional) to a group of alumni — you're auto-added |
| **Alumni office hours** | Drop-in sessions for debugging, career advice, or just chatting |
| **Career mentorship volunteering** | Alumni volunteer as career mentors for current interns |
| **AFE Bootcamp involvement** | Alumni teach concept-based sessions (build/deploy, unit tests, dev tools) |

### Stay connected after the program

- Subscribe to the [afe-alumni-interest@ mailing list](https://email-list.amazon.com/email-list/expand-list/afe-alumni-interest/) for program news and engagement opportunities
- Complete the [Alumni Interest Survey](https://amazonexteu.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_6VRYc1vqiZB2aBU) to indicate how you'd like to stay involved
- Opportunities include: career mentoring, bootcamp facilitation, Slack support, and office hours

## Intern recognition

AUTA celebrates contributions through monthly kudos and quarterly spotlights. Recognition nominations can come from managers, mentors, or peers. Check the [Intern Recognition Hall of Fame](https://w.amazon.com/bin/view/University/InternProgram/Interns/InternDay/) for past highlights.
