**GREF** (Global Real Estate & Facilities) handles equipment and workspace needs.

## What you can request

Standard equipment available to interns:

- **Monitors** — external displays (most teams provide one; you can request a second)
- **Peripherals** — keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam
- **Docking stations** — if your setup requires one
- **Ergonomic equipment** — standing desk converters, monitor arms, ergonomic keyboards

## How to request equipment

1. Go to **[gref.amazon.com](http://gref.amazon.com)**
2. Select **"Peripheral request"**
3. Choose from the approved equipment list
4. Submit your request

**Typical turnaround time:** 2-4 business days for standard peripherals like monitors.

## What's NOT available

- Personal laptops or tablets
- Non-standard equipment not on the approved list
- Equipment for home office (interns are in-office 5 days/week)

## Troubleshooting

If your request is delayed or denied, reach out to your manager first — they may need to approve it or help escalate.

For workspace issues (broken desk, seating problems, temperature), also use the GREF portal or contact your building's facilities team.
