**GREF** (Global Real Estate & Facilities) handles equipment and workspace needs.

## What you can request

Standard equipment available to interns:

- **Monitors** — external displays (most teams provide one; you can request a second)
- **Peripherals** — keyboard, mouse, headset, webcam
- **Docking station** — for connecting your laptop to monitors and peripherals
- **Chair upgrades** — ergonomic chair if the standard one doesn't work for you
- **Accessibility equipment** — standing desk converters, ergonomic accessories, assistive technology

Your **laptop** is provided on Day 1 (see [Before Day One](/docs/before-day-one)) — you don't need to request it through GREF.

## How to submit a request

1. **Go to A to Z** — [atoz.amazon.work](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "IT equipment" or "GREF"
2. **Submit a ticket** — select the equipment category and describe what you need
3. **Manager approval** — some items may need your manager's approval before fulfillment
4. **Pickup or delivery** — you'll receive instructions for where to collect your equipment (typically from an IT vending machine or stockroom in your building)

Expected turnaround: most standard items (monitors, peripherals) are available same-day from building stock. Special-order items may take 1–2 weeks.

### Tip: Expensing monitors through GREF

You can expense peripherals like monitors directly through GREF. Go to [gref.amazon.com](http://gref.amazon.com), select "peripheral request", and pick from the approved list. Typical delivery time is 3 days.

## Accessibility & accommodations

If you need specialized equipment for an accommodation:

1. Start with [MyHR](https://atoz.amazon.work/myhr/livehelp/landing) to document the accommodation request
2. HR will coordinate with GREF for equipment fulfillment
3. See [Health & Wellness](/docs/health-wellness) for the full accommodations process
