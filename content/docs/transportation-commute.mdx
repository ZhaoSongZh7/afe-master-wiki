All the ways to get to and around the workplace every day.

<Callout type="info">
  Interns are expected to be **in-office 5 days per week** at their assigned location, the same as full-time employees. Plan your commute accordingly.
</Callout>

## MyCommute — your commute planning tool

Amazon provides **MyCommute** to help you plan your route and access commute benefits:
- Route planning and real-time transit info
- Commute benefit enrollment
- Carpool matching

Access it at [mycommute.amazon.com](https://mycommute.amazon.com) (requires login).

## Shuttles

Amazon operates free shuttles between major hubs and buildings.

### Fremont to SLU (Seattle)

- **Peak hours (7-9 AM, 4-6 PM):** Every 15 minutes
- **Off-peak:** Every 30 minutes

Check the **internal transit page** (linked in MyCommute) for live schedules, route maps, and any service changes.

### Other routes

Shuttle routes vary by location. Check MyCommute or ask your team for the routes that serve your building.

## Public transit

Amazon offers subsidized public transit passes in most locations:

- **Seattle:** ORCA card subsidy
- **Bay Area:** Clipper card subsidy
- **Other locations:** check MyCommute for local options

Enroll through MyCommute to get the subsidy applied.

## Parking

Parking availability and cost vary by building:
- Some locations offer free parking
- Others require a paid parking pass (request through MyCommute)
- Some buildings have no on-site parking

Check with your manager or MyCommute before driving on Day 1.

## Lyft/rideshare

Amazon does not provide rideshare credits for daily commutes. Lyft/Uber are out-of-pocket unless you're traveling for work (in which case, expense through Concur).

## Biking

Most buildings have:
- Bike racks or bike rooms (badge access)
- Showers and lockers
- Bike repair stations

Check your building's amenities page or ask building services.

## Commute reimbursement

Interns are eligible for the same commute benefits as full-time employees. Enroll in MyCommute within your first week to start receiving subsidies.
