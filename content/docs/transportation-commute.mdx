All the ways to get to and around the workplace.

## Rideshare (Lyft)

If your site offers a program-provided rideshare benefit:

- Codes or credits are distributed by the program team at the start of the internship — check your welcome email or ask the [AFE Program Team](mailto:afe-internship-program@amazon.com)
- The benefit covers commute rides between your housing and the office, within posted limits
- Personal rides (evenings, weekends, non-work destinations) are **not** covered
- Reimbursement for other commute expenses is covered on [Expenses & Reimbursement](/docs/expenses-reimbursement)

## Parking

- Most corporate sites offer garage or lot parking — availability varies by building
- Register your vehicle through your site's parking portal (search "parking" on [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) for your building's process)
- Some urban sites (e.g., Seattle, Bellevue) have limited or paid parking — check with your onboarding buddy about what your building offers

## Public transit

- Many Amazon sites subsidize public transit passes (e.g., ORCA card in Puget Sound)
- Check [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "commute benefits" or "transit" for your location's offering
- Your onboarding buddy can tell you which lines and stops teammates actually use

## Shuttles

- Larger campuses run inter-building shuttles during business hours
- Schedules and routes are posted on A to Z and in building lobbies
- Ask your team which shuttle stops are closest to your building
