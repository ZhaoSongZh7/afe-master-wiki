## MyCommute — your commute planning tool

Amazon provides **MyCommute** to help you find the best way to get to work:

- Access via [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "MyCommute"
- Shows available transportation options for your building (transit, shuttles, bike, parking)
- Displays parking availability and transit routes specific to your office location

Use MyCommute as your first stop when planning your daily commute, especially for events like Intern Day/Week when parking may be limited.

## Rideshare (Lyft)

Some intern packages include a Lyft commute benefit. If your offer or program includes Lyft credits:

### How it works

- **Credits/codes** are distributed by the AFE or AUTA program team, typically in your welcome communications or on Day 1
- **Eligible rides**: commute between your housing and your assigned office
- **Not covered**: personal rides (evenings, weekends, non-work destinations, airport trips)
- **Limits**: credit amounts and ride caps vary by location — check your specific benefit details

### Setting up Lyft

1. Download the **Lyft app** on your phone
2. Create an account (or use your existing personal account)
3. Apply the Amazon credit code provided to you (instructions come with the code)
4. Request rides to/from your office as normal — credits are applied automatically

### If you don't have Lyft credits

- Check with your manager or the [AFE Program Team](mailto:afe-internship-program@amazon.com) to confirm whether your location offers this benefit
- If not available, your commute expenses are typically your responsibility (not reimbursable for regular daily commuting)

## Parking

### How to get a parking spot

- Check availability through **MyCommute** (via A to Z) for your specific building
- Some buildings have **garage parking** with badge-activated entry
- Other buildings use **lot parking** requiring a permit or registration
- Register your vehicle through your site's parking system (search "parking" on A to Z for your building)

### Key details by location type

| Location type | Parking situation |
| --- | --- |
| **Urban/downtown** (Seattle, NYC, Arlington) | Limited and often paid. Consider transit alternatives. |
| **Suburban campus** (Nashville, Austin, Detroit) | Generally available, free lot parking common. |
| **Mixed** (Denver, Bay Area) | Varies by specific building. Check MyCommute. |

### Tips

- Ask your **onboarding buddy** about the parking situation on Day 1 — they'll know the real story (which lots fill up early, where overflow parking is, etc.)
- Some sites have EV charging stations — check MyCommute for availability
- Motorcycle/bike parking is usually available and often closer to building entrances

## Public transit

Amazon supports public transit use in many locations:

| Location | Transit system | Amazon benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seattle / Puget Sound** | Link Light Rail, Metro Bus | ORCA card subsidy (check A to Z → "commute benefits") |
| **Arlington, VA (HQ2)** | DC Metro (Blue/Yellow lines) | SmartBenefits / transit subsidy |
| **New York, NY** | Subway (MTA) | Pre-tax transit benefit |
| **San Francisco Bay Area** | BART, Muni, Caltrain | Commuter benefit program |
| **Other locations** | Varies | Check A to Z → "commute benefits" for your site |

To find your specific transit benefit:
1. Go to [A to Z](https://atoz.amazon.work/) → search "commute benefits" or "transit"
2. Select your office location
3. Review available subsidies and how to enroll

## Shuttles

Larger Amazon campuses operate **inter-building shuttles**:

- **Amazon Ride** (Seattle/Bellevue) — connects major Amazon buildings; badge-activated; real-time tracking via app
- **Campus shuttles** (other locations) — routes and schedules posted in building lobbies and on A to Z
- **Ask your team** which shuttle stops are closest to your building

Shuttle schedules typically run during business hours (7 AM – 7 PM). Check A to Z or your building's lobby for current routes and frequency.

### Example: Fremont to SLU shuttle (Seattle)

The shuttle from Fremont to South Lake Union runs:
- **Peak hours** (7–9 AM, 4–6 PM): every 15 minutes
- **Off-peak**: every 30 minutes

Check the internal transit page for real-time schedules and route maps.

## Biking

Many Amazon offices support bike commuting:

- **Bike cages/rooms** — secure indoor storage (badge access)
- **Bike racks** — outdoor racks near building entrances
- **Showers** — most larger buildings have locker rooms with showers
- **Bike repair stations** — available at some campuses

Check MyCommute for bike facilities at your specific building.

## Commute during intern events

For special events (Intern Day/Week, team offsites):

- **Parking may be extra limited** — use MyCommute to check availability and consider alternative transportation
- **Bring your badge** — required for building entry at any Amazon location
- **Plan extra time** — large events mean more people arriving simultaneously
