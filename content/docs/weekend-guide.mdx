Where to go and what to do when you're off the clock. A starting point, not a
rulebook — go explore.

## Coffee (it's Seattle, after all)

- **Espresso Vivace** (Capitol Hill) — often called the city's best espresso.
- **Victrola** and **Analog** — great Capitol Hill roasters to work or read in.
- **Storyville Coffee** (Pike Place) — beautiful space with a market view.
- The **original Starbucks** at Pike Place — touristy, but a fun one-time stop.

## Cheap eats

- **Pike Place Market** — piroshky, chowder, fresh fruit, and people-watching.
- **The U-District** — the best value in the city; global food on a student budget.
- **Dick's Drive-In** — a Seattle institution; late-night burgers.
- **International District** — dim sum, pho, bao, and bubble tea.

## Free (or nearly free) things

- **Discovery Park** — beaches, bluffs, a lighthouse, and skyline views.
- **Olympic Sculpture Park** — free waterfront art with mountain views.
- **Gas Works Park** — the classic Lake Union + skyline photo spot.
- **Fremont** — the troll, quirky shops, and Sunday market.
- **Kerry Park** — the postcard sunset view of the skyline and Rainier.

## Rainy-day options

Seattle summers are mostly dry, but when it turns:

- **Seattle Art Museum (SAM)** and the **Chihuly Garden & Glass**.
- **Museum of Pop Culture (MoPOP)** — music, sci-fi, and pop culture.
- **The Central Library** — a stunning building, worth a visit on its own.
- **Bookstores + a coffee shop** — the most Seattle rainy afternoon there is.
